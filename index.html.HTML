ABOUT US

The Hindu College of Engineering came into existence in 1999. The Institute is duly approved by AICTE and is affiliated to Deenbandhu Chhotu Ram University of Science & Technology, Murthal, Sonepat. (http://www.dcrustm.ac.in)
The Institute is running 3 year Full-time Diploma in five disciplines viz. CSE, ECE, EE, ME & CE and Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA). The Institute also offers 4 year Full-time B.Tech. programs in five disciplines viz. CSE, ECE, EE, and 2 year Full-time M.Tech. programs in 3 disciplines viz. CSE, ECE & EE (Power Systems).
The opulent infrastructure of the College is spread over 10 acres of land. The Institute is well equipped with Laboratories, Workshops, Conference/Seminar Halls, spacious Classrooms, Cafeteria, Hostel accommodation (separate for boys and girls) etc.
The College has a well stocked Library with more than 20,000 books (book-bank facility also available) and more than 200 International/National Journals (OnLine/Print Access) and Magazines. Access to E-Journals like IEEE, JGATE, SPRINGER, ASCE, TATA McGRAW etc. is available to the students and staff members in the Library round the clock.

Courses

1. B.TECH

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) is a four-year undergraduate engineering programme.
It focuses on advanced technical knowledge, practical learning, problem-solving and innovation. Students can pursue specializations such as:
  • Computer Science and Engineering:
    • Programming, software development, databases and computing technologies.
  • Civil Engineering:
    • Construction, buildings, roads, bridges and structural design.
  • Electrical Engineering:
    • Electrical circuits, power systems, machines and electrical equipment.
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering:
    • Electronic devices, communication systems and embedded technologies.
  • Mechanical Engineering:
    • Machines, manufacturing, thermodynamics and mechanical design.

2. BCA

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is a three-year undergraduate programme focused on computer applications and information technology. Students learn programming languages, computer fundamentals, database management, web development and software concepts.
The programme develops logical thinking, problem-solving and technical skills.
It is suitable for students interested in careers in software development, web development, IT support and other computer-related fields.

3. DIPLOMA

The Diploma in Engineering is a three-year technical programme designed to provide students with basic engineering knowledge and practical skills.
Students learn through classroom teaching, laboratory experiments and workshop activities.
The programme offers specializations such as Civil, Computer, Electrical, Electronics and Communication, and Mechanical Engineering.
It prepares students for technical jobs and further engineering studies.

4. M.TECH

The Master of Technology (M.Tech) is a postgraduate programme designed for students who want to gain advanced knowledge and specialization in engineering. It focuses on technical expertise, research, innovation and advanced problem-solving.
Listed specializations include Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Electrical Engineering, including Power Systems.
It helps students prepare for specialized engineering roles, research and further academic studies. Careers360 +1 Official website:

The College has well furnished separate Hostels for both boys and girls students with excellent residential facilities. The hostels have the facility of Gym, lush green lawns and Wi-Fi connectivity. Placement

1. Message From Placement Officer

The Training and Placement Cell of Hindu College of Engineering helps students prepare for their professional careers. It guides students in developing technical knowledge, communication skills, confidence and interview skills. The cell aims to connect students with industry opportunities and support them in finding suitable internships and employment.

2. Internship Policy

Internships help students gain practical experience and understand how industries work. Through internship opportunities, students can apply classroom knowledge to real-world tasks, develop technical and professional skills, and understand workplace expectations. Students should follow the college's applicable internship guidelines and complete the required training and documentation.

3. Placement Record

The Placement Record section provides information about students' placement achievements and employment opportunities. It helps students learn about the career outcomes of previous batches and understand the importance of academic performance, technical skills, aptitude preparation and interview readiness. For accurate placement statistics, students should refer to the latest records published by the college.

4. Industrial Visits & Tie-ups

Hindu College of Engineering promotes practical learning through industrial visits and industry-related collaborations. These activities allow students to observe workplace practices, learn about new technologies and understand real industrial processes. The official website mentions visits by ECE and EE students to ATAL Academy and IDEA Lab at DCRUST, Murthal. The college also lists industrial tie-ups with the Institute for Industrial Development (IID) and Study International Education Consultants (SIEC).

5. Our Recruiters

The Our Recruiters section highlights organizations associated with recruitment opportunities for students of Hindu College of Engineering. Recruiters can provide opportunities for internships and employment according to students' qualifications, skills and organizational requirements. Students are encouraged to strengthen their technical knowledge, communication skills and problem-solving abilities to prepare for recruitment processes.
